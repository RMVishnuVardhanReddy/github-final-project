# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in our project and community a respectful, welcoming, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, background, experience level, identity, nationality, race, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability, or personal beliefs.

We are committed to creating an open, friendly, and collaborative environment where everyone can contribute safely and respectfully.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Treating others with respect and professionalism.
- Being open to different opinions and experiences.
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback.
- Using welcoming and inclusive language.
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and community.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, threats, intimidation, or discrimination.
- Insulting, offensive, or abusive comments.
- Personal or political attacks.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Unwanted sexual attention or inappropriate behavior.
- Deliberately disrupting discussions, development, or community activities.
- Any other behavior that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional environment.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for defining and enforcing acceptable standards of behavior.

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, pull requests, documentation, or other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct.

Maintainers may also temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces, including:

- Source code repositories
- Issues and pull requests
- Discussions and comments
- Documentation
- Project-related communication channels
- Meetings and events

It also applies when an individual is officially representing the project in public or private spaces.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior should be reported to the project maintainers.

All complaints will be reviewed carefully and handled as confidentially as reasonably possible.

Project maintainers will investigate reported incidents and take appropriate action based on the circumstances.

Anyone responsible for enforcing this Code of Conduct must avoid conflicts of interest and handle reports fairly.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Project maintainers may use the following guidelines when determining the consequences of a violation.

### 1. Correction

**Community Impact:** Minor inappropriate behavior or unprofessional language.

**Consequence:** A private warning explaining the violation and requesting that the behavior be corrected.

### 2. Warning

**Community Impact:** A repeated violation or a more serious incident.

**Consequence:** A formal warning. The individual may be asked to stop certain interactions or activities for a specified period.

### 3. Temporary Ban

**Community Impact:** Serious or repeated violations that negatively affect the community.

**Consequence:** Temporary removal from project discussions, contributions, communication channels, or other community activities.

### 4. Permanent Ban

**Community Impact:** Persistent violations, harassment, threats, abuse, or behavior that creates an unsafe environment.

**Consequence:** Permanent removal from participation in the project and its community spaces.
